# Pathmend — Demo Screencast Script

A ~90-second walkthrough for the listing video / review submission. Record from
the embedded admin with demo data seeded. Keep it tight: show value fast, no
dead air. No myshopify.com URLs, no PII on screen.

**Total target: 75–90 seconds.**

---

### 0:00–0:08 — Hook (Dashboard)

> "When you migrate a store, links break — and every broken link is a lost
> visitor. Pathmend catches them and fixes them for you."

- On screen: the Dashboard, stat cards showing unresolved 404s and redirects
  created. Cursor rests on "Unresolved 404s".

### 0:08–0:25 — The 404 log + one-click fix (core)

> "Pathmend tracks 404s automatically as visitors hit them. Here's a broken
> product URL getting traffic. One click…"

- Navigate to **404 Log**. Point at a row with a high hit count.
- Click **Fix**. The redirect form is pre-filled with the broken path.
- Type the destination, click **Create redirect**. Toast: "Redirect created."

> "…and it's a real Shopify redirect. No proxy, no slowdown, and it won't
> silently disappear."

### 0:25–0:45 — Migration importer (differentiator)

> "Migrating a whole store? Point Pathmend at your old site's sitemap."

- Navigate to **Migrate**. Paste a sitemap URL, click **Fetch & match**.
- The review table fills with Old URL → Matched to, with confidence badges.

> "It matches every old URL to the right product, collection, or page — and
> scores its confidence so you review before anything goes live. Approve the
> good ones, apply them all at once."

- Click **Create N redirects**. Toast confirms.

### 0:45–1:00 — Pattern rules & auto-heal (differentiator)

> "For whole families of URLs, write one rule."

- Navigate to **Pattern rules**. Show a `/blog/*` → `/news/*` rule.
- In the preview field type `/blog/my-old-post` → shows "Would redirect to
  /news/my-old-post".

> "Now, whenever a visitor hits a matching broken link, Pathmend creates the
> exact redirect for it — automatically."

### 1:00–1:15 — Analytics (value/retention)

> "And you can see the impact."

- Navigate to **Analytics**. Show the 404s-over-time chart and the summary
  cards.

> "404s over time, your most-missed URLs, and an estimate of the visits you've
> won back by fixing them."

### 1:15–1:25 — Close

> "Pathmend. Catch every 404, fix it in one click, and never lose a visitor to a
> dead link again."

- End on the Dashboard or the Pathmend wordmark.

---

## Production notes

- Seed demo data first (varied paths, hit counts, a populated 30-day chart, a
  few products/collections/pages so the matcher finds real matches).
- Use a test subscription so paid features (Migrate, Pattern rules, Analytics)
  render instead of the upsell.
- Keep the cursor deliberate; pause ~1s after each toast so it's readable.
- No pricing on screen, no myshopify.com URL in the admin frame, no real
  customer data.
- Record at 1080p minimum; the listing accepts up to 1920×1080.
